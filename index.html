01 / GETTING TO KNOW ME

hello!

I'm Kadie, a Computer Science and Human-Computer Interaction student at Carnegie Mellon University.

I'm interested in both building technology and understanding how people actually experience it. That has led me toward work involving programming, human-centered research, artificial intelligence, product design, and user experience.

I enjoy taking complicated problems and turning them into something thoughtful, useful, and approachable.

I also value collaboration, communication, leadership, and learning from people who approach problems differently than I do.
