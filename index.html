hey, I’m Kadie

CS student, HCI thinker, human-centered builder

I’m a Carnegie Mellon student studying Computer Science and Human-Computer Interaction. I’m especially passionate about human behavior and technology, and that interest is what inspired me to move toward the field of human-centered technology.

I love work that combines creativity, research, design, and technical problem solving. I’m most interested in understanding how people think, what makes an experience feel intuitive, and how technology can be designed around real human needs.
