I'm Kadie, a Computer Science and Human-Computer Interaction student at Carnegie Mellon University.

I love exploring the space where technology, people, and design overlap. I'm interested in both building technology and understanding how people actually experience and interact with it.

Through my coursework and projects, I've explored programming, computer systems, artificial intelligence, human-centered research, product design, and user experience.

I enjoy taking complicated problems and turning them into experiences that feel thoughtful, approachable, and useful.
