after i uploaded my photos when i click on the subsection they take a second to load do you know why that might be


Yeah — that’s pretty common, especially after replacing placeholders with real photos. The most likely cause is that the images are simply much larger than the placeholder content was.
A few things can cause the delay:
* Large image file sizes. Phone photos can easily be 3–10+ MB each. For a portfolio, most images can usually be around 200–500 KB without looking noticeably worse.
* Huge image dimensions. An iPhone photo might be 4000px wide even though your website only displays it at 500–1000px.
* loading="lazy" on images. If your subsection is hidden until you click it, the browser may wait until then to actually load the photo.
* CSS background images in hidden sections. Those sometimes aren't fetched until the section becomes visible, which creates that little pause.
* First visit vs. repeat visit. The first time an image loads, the browser has to download it. After that it may feel much faster because it's cached.
What I would do
First, resize your portfolio photos to roughly 1200–1600px on the longest side and convert them to WebP if possible. That usually makes the biggest difference.
Then, if your HTML has something like:
<img src="images/alpha-phi.jpg" loading="lazy">
and that image appears immediately after someone clicks a subsection, change it to:
<img src="images/alpha-phi.jpg" loading="eager">
Or just:
<img src="images/alpha-phi.jpg">
For the photos inside your clickable subsections, another really good solution is to preload them when the website opens so they're already ready by the time someone clicks.
Add this inside your <head>:
<link rel="preload" as="image" href="images/photo1.webp">
<link rel="preload" as="image" href="images/photo2.webp">
<link rel="preload" as="image" href="images/photo3.webp">
You don't necessarily want to preload every photo if you have a ton, but for the handful used in the clickable About/Outside the Classroom sections, it makes sense.
If the delay is only happening the first time you click each section and then it's instant afterward, that's a very strong sign that the browser is loading the image only when that section opens.
